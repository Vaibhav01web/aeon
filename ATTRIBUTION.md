on derived databases — if you publish derived data, license it ODbL and
attribute.

Weather © Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0 · Routing © Project-OSRM, BSD-2
Norms: CPHEEO SLB Handbook (MoHUA), CPCB MSW Annual Report 2021-22, CEA General Review 2025

**Licence-compatibility note.** Google Open Buildings is dual-licensed
CC BY 4.0 OR ODbL 1.0, but the VIDA merge includes OSM, so the merged
product is ODbL and share-alike applies to your derived database. Ship
ODbL and attribute properly.

note it if this is ever productised.
